Company Information

Transforming Corporate Visions Into Tangible Media

Operating from Anamnagar-32, Kathmandu, Swabhiman Multipurpose Private Limited is a full-service creative press and agency. We specialize in high-precision commercial printing, custom product packaging, outdoor advertising media, and modern digital marketing.

Equipped with state-of-the-art CTP plates, offset machinery, and digital presses, we ensure every project delivers flawless ink consistency, crisp text, and vibrant color accuracy across all paper stocks.
